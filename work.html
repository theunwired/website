Work

Case studies go up when the client has measured the result.

Not when the build ships, and not a week earlier. Each one goes up after the client has signed off on it and put a number to it themselves. Until a case study is here, the Builds page shows what we can demo on your own documents, and the notes below show how we think about the work.

01 The template

What every entry on this page will carry

Every case study on this page gets built against these five things. If it can't fill all five, it doesn't go up. This is the rule, published, so you can hold us to it.

01 The named process

Not "finance automation". The actual process, the systems it crosses, the volume it runs at.

02 What was breaking

The failure mode before we arrived, in the words the team used, including what they had already tried.

03 The guardrails we set

Tolerances, escalation points, and what we deliberately left to a human. The interesting part.

04 One real number

Hours, cycle time, or exception rate. Measured before and after, by the client, not by us.

05 What we got wrong

Every build has a week where something misfires. It goes in the write-up.
