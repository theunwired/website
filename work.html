Any document. One workflow.

A document comes in. Data gets pulled out. Where the model is confident, it moves straight into the process. Where it isn't, a person sees only the fields in question, in a review window built for that, not the raw file. This is the first engagement we're documenting on this page, live, as it runs.

01

KYC & onboarding

Evidence work only

Identity documents, proof of address, and screening results in a dozen formats from a dozen jurisdictions. The agent reads them, checks them against each other, and builds the file. A person clears the case.

  • ID and address documents read and cross-checked for name and date mismatches
  • Screening evidence assembled into the case file with the source of every match
02

Invoices & AP documents

Highest volume

Line items, tax codes and PO references pulled from any layout, not just the templates a vendor happened to standardise on. What comes out feeds the match, not a spreadsheet someone still has to check.

  • Header and line-item fields extracted regardless of vendor layout
  • Extracted data pushed straight into the P2P match, no re-keying step
03

Contracts

Strong fit

Renewal dates, obligations and pricing terms buried in free text across hundreds of contracts. The agent reads them once and keeps a system of record updated as amendments land.

  • Key terms and renewal dates extracted and tracked against a calendar
  • Amendments matched to the original clause, not filed as a new document
04

Anything with a workflow behind it

Ask us

Claim forms, correspondence, applications, handwritten notes, a mix of structured fields and free text on the same page. If there's a document and a process it should feed, the pipeline is the same. We build it to your document, not the other way round.

01 How it works

A validation window on the fields that need one, not a queue of documents to check.

1 Any format in

A scan, a photograph, a native PDF, a structured form. The pipeline reads the document as it actually arrives.

2 Extract and score

Every field comes out with a confidence score, not a single pass-fail on the whole document.

3 A person checks the low-confidence fields

Only the fields the model flagged, shown next to the source document. Nothing else needs a second look.

4 It moves forward

Confirmed data is pushed into the target system, and the correction becomes training signal for the next document like it.

Treat that last row carefully, including when we say it. A 99% accuracy claim at character level is a completely different number from 99% at record level, and vendors rarely say which one they mean. At record level, 99% still means one in a hundred documents needs correcting. The meta-analysis above is peer-reviewed across 93 papers. The accuracy row is vendor-aggregated and should be read as direction of travel, not as a promise.

Never evaluate extraction on clean PDFs. Every tool looks good on a well-formatted invoice. Test it on the faxed copy, the handwritten form, the one where the total is buried in a table footnote. That is where the differences show up, and it is how we scope. Send us your worst documents, not your best ones.
