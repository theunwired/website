Privacy

What this site collects, and what we do with it.

This page covers theunwired.in and the contact form on it. Data we handle for a client during an engagement is covered by that contract and by our Security page.

Last updated 15 October 2026

01 What we collect

Only what you send us.

01 The contact form

Your name, your email address and the message you write. Nothing else is recorded from the form.

02 Email

Anything you send to us directly, including attachments.

03 No tracking

This site has no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking cookies. It sets no cookies of its own.

02 Who else sees it

The services this site runs on.

01 Form delivery

The contact form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a form-to-email service.

02 Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

03 Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

03 What we do with it

And what we never do.

01 We use it to reply

We use what you send to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation.

02 We don't sell it

We don't sell or share it, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask to be on one.

03 How long we keep it

We delete it when you ask, or 12 months after the conversation ends if you do not become a client.

04 Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to theunwired.in@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If we start collecting something new, it will be listed here before we do.
