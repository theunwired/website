02 Why it breaks

It was hard-wired, and the work moved.

Old automation follows the wiring you gave it. One path, one list of exceptions, and it stops the moment something new turns up. An agent follows the goal instead.

Wired automation

  • If this, then that. Nothing else
  • Stops on anything it has not seen before
  • Every exception needs a developer
  • Runs the steps, never makes the call

Unwired agents

  • Works toward a goal, using what is in front of it
  • Handles the odd case without a rewrite
  • Learns from exceptions, passes on the rest
  • Plans, acts, and checks its own work
