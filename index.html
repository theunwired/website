The shift

From wired to unwired

Traditional automation follows the wiring you gave it — one path, one exception list, one break when the input changes. Agentic AI follows the goal instead.

Wired automation

  • ×Scripted, if-this-then-that logic
  • ×Breaks on unfamiliar inputs or UI changes
  • ×Every exception needs a developer
  • ×Bots that execute, but don't decide

Unwired agents

  • ✓Goal-directed reasoning over live context
  • ✓Handles variation without a rewrite
  • ✓Learns from exceptions, escalates the rest
  • ✓Agents that plan, act, and check their own work
Our services

One loop, run by the agent

Every Unwired deployment runs the same underlying cycle — tuned to your process, your systems, your risk tolerance.

01 · Understand

Perceive

Agents read documents, screens, emails, and systems the way a person would — no rigid field mapping required.

02 · Decide

Reason

Given a goal and your business rules, the agent plans its own next step instead of following a fixed script.

03 · Execute

Act

It operates your applications and APIs directly, and knows when to pause and bring a human in.

04 · Improve

Learn

Every exception and correction feeds back in, so the agent gets sharper on your process over time.

Engagement

How we work with you

Week 1

Map the wiring

We shadow the process as it's actually run today — not the flowchart version — and find where it breaks.

Week 2–3

Design the agent

We define the agent's goal, guardrails, and escalation points with the team who owns the process.

Week 4–6

Deploy in parallel

The agent runs alongside the existing process, building trust before it takes the lead.

Ongoing

Scale and tune

We extend the agent to adjacent workflows and keep tuning it against real exceptions.
