Revenue operations

The updates nobody has time to make.

The CRM is rarely wrong because people are careless. It's wrong because updating it is someone's fourth priority. Quote to cash breaks in the gaps between sales, finance and delivery, wherever a record gets copied by hand from one system to the next.

All industries
01

CRM hygiene

Highest volume

Meeting notes, emails and calls hold the facts the CRM is missing. The agent reads them and proposes the update. The rep confirms it.

  • Next steps, stakeholders and dates proposed from the activity record
  • Duplicate accounts and contacts flagged, never merged automatically
  • Stale opportunities surfaced to the owner with what changed
02

Order handoff

Strong fit

A signed order form has to become a billing record, a provisioning request and a project. The agent reads the contract and creates each one, with every term traced to its clause.

  • Contract terms extracted and pushed into the billing record
  • Non-standard terms flagged to finance before the first invoice
  • Handoff to delivery created with the scope as signed
03

Billing reconciliation

Strong fit

Usage, contracts and invoices drift apart. The agent compares them every cycle and flags what was under-billed, over-billed or never billed at all.

  • Invoices checked against contract terms and usage
  • Discrepancies routed to the account owner with the evidence
  • Credits and corrections prepared, never issued on their own
04

Renewal preparation

Strong fit

Before a renewal conversation, the agent assembles usage, support history, open issues and the original terms, so the account manager starts with facts.

  • Renewal brief assembled ahead of the date you set
  • Risk signals listed with their source
01 How the agent works

The agent proposes the update. The owner of the record confirms it.

1 Something happens

A contract is signed, a call is logged, an invoice run finishes.

2 It reads the record

The agent reads the contract, the notes or the usage file and finds what the other systems are missing.

3 It proposes the change

The CRM update, the billing record or the correction, staged with the source attached.

4 The owner confirms

The rep, the billing lead or the account manager approves. Then every system updates together.
