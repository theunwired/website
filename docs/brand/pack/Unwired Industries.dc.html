Where it runs

Built for the processes that keep breaking.

We don't sell by vertical. We sell by process. These are the functions where the work is high-volume, exception-heavy, and spread across systems that were never built to talk.

01

Finance & accounting

Highest fit

Invoice exceptions, reconciliations, and close-cycle work that used to sit in someone's queue. The rules already exist as tolerances, which is exactly what an agent needs to act safely.

Three-way match with variance tolerances your finance lead sets Vendor query drafting and follow-up, escalated when it stalls Month-end schedules assembled and flagged, never posted blind
02

Supply chain

Highest fit

Order matching, vendor communication, and shipment exceptions handled without a ticket. Most of the delay here is waiting for a human to read an email and update a second system.

Order acknowledgements reconciled against the PO automatically Delay notices read, impact assessed, the right planner told Short shipments raised with evidence attached, not a blank form
03

Customer operations

Strong fit

Multi-step case resolution across systems that were never built to talk to each other. Not a chatbot on the front. The work behind the ticket, done properly.

Case triaged, context gathered from every system before a human sees it Refunds and credits prepared to policy, approved by a person Follow-ups chased until the case actually closes
04

Revenue operations

Strong fit

Quote-to-cash steps that stall between sales, finance and delivery. The CRM is rarely wrong because people are careless. It's wrong because updating it is someone's fourth priority.

Contract terms extracted and pushed into the billing record Renewal and usage signals assembled before the QBR, not during it
05

Compliance & audit

Evidence work only

Gathering evidence, not making the judgement. The agent assembles the pack and cites every source. A person signs it. That line does not move.

Control evidence collected on schedule with the trail attached Exceptions surfaced early instead of during the audit window
Where we say no

Anything where the process is undocumented and contested, where the decision carries legal exposure, or where a scheduled job would fix it for a fraction of the cost. We would rather lose the engagement than build the wrong thing well.
