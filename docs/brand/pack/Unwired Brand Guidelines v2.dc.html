01 Positioning
POSITIONING STATEMENT

The Unwired is an AI automation studio for operators. We don't advise on AI. We build the working system, tailored to your process, and hand you the keys.

CATEGORY

AI process automation studio. Not a consultancy, not an advisory, not a SaaS product.

GEOGRAPHY

Primary market: US and North America. Delivery team of three senior founders, no junior bench.

WHO IT'S FOR

Founders of mid-size companies, and the functional managers who own a process that keeps breaking. Finance ops, rev ops, supply chain, customer ops, compliance.

THE PROMISE

You describe the process. We hand back a configurable, documented system your team owns, maintains, and scales without us.

Internal note: the years-of-experience framing lives in this document and in conversation, never on the website. Externally we show the work, not the CV.

03 Voice & tone

Blunt, specific, unimpressed by AI. We sound like the person in the room who has actually done the work.

V1Say the thing plainly

Short declaratives. No hedging, no "we believe", no "in today's landscape". If a sentence survives losing five words, lose them.

DO"Your invoice exceptions sit in a queue. An agent can clear them."
DON'T"We help organisations unlock transformative efficiency through intelligent automation."
V2Be specific, or say nothing

Name the process, the system, the exception, the hour saved. A claim with no noun in it is bloat. Never invent a number.

DO"Order matching across three systems that were never built to talk."
DON'T"End-to-end intelligent process orchestration at enterprise scale."
V3Contrarian where it's earned

We're allowed to disagree with the category: with demos, decks, and AI as a shopping list. Punch at the practice, never at the reader. Confidence, not contempt.

DO"Most AI pilots die in the demo. We don't run demos."
DON'T"If your team is still doing this manually, you're already behind."
V4Honest about limits

Saying what we won't do is the strongest thing we say. Name the cases where an agent is the wrong tool. Never overclaim autonomy.

DO"Tell us about the process that keeps breaking. We'll tell you honestly whether an agent is the right fix."
DON'T"Fully autonomous agents that handle any workflow, out of the box."

UNTANGLED · THE DEFAULT MARK
theunwired
ON BONE · PRINT, EMAIL

The mark is two wires. Each runs in flat and horizontal, bends once, crosses the centre and runs out flat again. Square-cut ends, mitred corners, no taper, stroke weight equal to the wordmark's stems. Crossed, they form the ✕ in theun✕wired: the hard-wired state. Pulled straight and stacked, a full white wire over a shorter green one, they become the default mark, and that untangled form is what appears everywhere the logo is static.

BEAT 1 · 0-700MSBoth bends flatten; the wires straighten and separate. The ✕ becomes two parallel lines, still sitting inside the word.
BEAT 2 · 700-1400MSThe pair leaves the word and lands at the front as the mark; the green wire shortens to 58%; the word closes up and the grey "theun" resolves to white, reading "theunwired".
BEAT 3 · 1400-2500MSThe sweep: a soft green band travels left to right through the letterforms once, current arriving, and the wordmark settles plain Bone. No flicker, no second pass.
TRIGGERBeats 1-2 are scroll-linked across the first 60% of viewport height, so scrubbing back re-tangles the mark. Beat 3 fires on completion, once per visit. Header logo stays untangled and never animates again.
REDUCED MOTIONprefers-reduced-motion renders the untangled lockup, static, with no sweep. Same for email, print, and any export.
THE WIRE FIELD · THE MARK, PERFORMED BY THE WHOLE PAGE

Ten wires run behind every page in the same grammar as the mark, drawn as smooth curves with no corners anywhere. Knotted at the top of a page, gathered through one bundle as they resolve, and combed into a working process flow by the foot of it. Dashes travel along every wire and signal blips ride the paths, so current is always running. One wire is green. Content panels are opaque and punch through the field, so nothing ever runs behind body copy.

TANGLEDCrossings at nine stations across the full width, a different permutation at each, so no two line up. Never one knot in one place.
GATHEREDMid-resolve every wire pulls toward one line behind the mark, then releases. It reads as a bundle, never as a single collapsed line.
RESOLVEDSquare process steps, diamond decision points, four sparse cross-links. A blip passing within 34px lights a node, then it goes quiet. This is the only place intelligence is depicted, and it is depicted as a process, never as a brain, a robot or a particle cloud.
DRIVEN BYHome: the field follows the logo's untangle through the pinned hero and stays resolved afterwards. Every other page: its own scroll progress, top to bottom. Never both, they fight.
WEIGHTStrokes 1.25px at #1E211B, the green wire 1.7px at #17493C. It must read as structure at the edge of vision, never as a graphic competing with the type.
MOTION · REVISED, SUPERSEDES V1
REVEALSOpacity and 10px translate, 450ms, ease-out. Once per element.
PANEL SWEEPA hairline green sweep travels across a bordered panel as it lands, 850ms. Re-arms once the panel has fully left the viewport, so it plays on every pass, not once ever.
BREATHINGSignal accents shift brightness on one shared 7s clock. Never per element, never out of phase.
PAGE TRANSITIONA Cut Black panel with a 2px Signal leading edge wipes in, 300ms, then out on arrival, 400ms. Links prefetch on hover. It reads as the cut, never as a fade.
STILL BANNEDBounce, elastic easing, parallax layers, anything that scales on hover beyond a 4px lift, decorative particles, and any motion that does not carry meaning.
REDUCED MOTIONEverything above is off. The field renders once, resolved and static. Content is visible with no reveal.

Rules: the wordmark is lowercase, one word, always Instrument Sans Bold at -4% tracking, and "the" is never dropped. Tangled ✕ above 80px and in motion only. Never frozen into a favicon, avatar or signature. Clear space equals the height of the gap between the two wires. Never outline the mark, never rotate it, never place it on a photograph, never recolour the white wire. Favicon and avatar: the untangled mark, left-aligned, on Forest or Cut Black.

Two typefaces, no exceptions. Instrument Sans carries everything structural, in Bold for display and headings and Regular for body. IBM Plex Mono carries labels, step numbers, metrics and anything that should read as machine output. Mono is never used for body copy. Italics are never used. Both are open source and free for commercial use, so the site, the deck and a client PDF all render in the real thing with no licence to buy. Web stack: Instrument Sans, then Helvetica Neue, then Arial.

LAYOUT PRINCIPLES
12-column grid, 64px gutters, hard left alignment. Nothing centred except a logo on a cover. Structure is drawn with 1px hairlines and 1px grid gaps, never with shadows, glows or rounded corners. Radius is 0 everywhere, buttons, cards, inputs, images. Spacing steps: 8 / 12 / 20 / 32 / 56 / 100. Section padding is 100px vertical. Mono eyebrow above every section, numbered. The numbering is part of the identity. Whitespace is the only luxury signal. When a page feels thin, cut content, don't add ornament.
IMAGERY, ICONS, MOTION
No stock photography of people at laptops. No robots, no glowing brains, no circuit-board textures. Preferred imagery: real screenshots of what we built, redacted; process diagrams drawn in the hairline grid. Icons: none. Where an icon is tempting, use a mono step number instead. Diagrams are boxes, hairlines and one green stroke for the agent's path. Motion: see the motion section below. The old rule (opacity and 8px translate only, logo untangle once per visit) was written for a brochure and is superseded. Charts: single green series on hairline axes, value labels in mono.
05 Applications
SITE STRUCTURE · THE BUILT ARC, HOME PAGE

The page does not list what we do. It resolves something. The reader arrives at a knot and leaves at a working process, and the wire field carries that literally. Every beat is a step in the untangling, so nothing can be reordered without breaking the argument.

01HeroThe knot. Mark, primary line, buyer eyebrow. Pinned, scroll-scrubbed. The field is fully tangled and untangles with the mark.
02The costName what the tangle costs before claiming anything. Month-end scramble, the tool bought but still done by hand, nobody can say where the work is. Written in the reader’s words, no product language.
03Why it breaksWired versus unwired, hairline columns. The diagnosis, not the pitch.
04What changesThe agent run. A real AP exception moves read, decide, act, escalate, with a live log beside it. The turn in the story and the strongest thing on the site. It must escalate: the honest stop is the point.
05Who does itThree operators by function, not by name. Credibility lands after we have shown we understand the problem, never before.
06What you getThe deliverable. Agent running, guardrails you set, full audit trail.
07Where it runsSix functions. Specificity beats coverage, cut as real work arrives.
08How it goesSix weeks, week by week. Most credible section for a functional manager.
09You own itThe closing beat, full width, display scale. “We build it. You own it.” The differentiator gets its own chapter, never a bullet.
10ContactThe honest-answer CTA. Modal, same on every page.

Inner pages (What you get, Industries, Work) run the same chapter grammar with their own numbering and their own wire arc.
