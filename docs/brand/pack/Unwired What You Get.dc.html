02Limits

What we won't do

If an agent is the wrong tool for the process you bring us, we say so in the first conversation. It is cheaper for everyone.

Processes nobody has written down

If the rules only live in one person's head, we map it first. That is week one, not a surprise in week five.

Decisions that must stay human

Hiring, pricing, anything with legal exposure. The agent prepares the case. A person still calls it.

One-off work a script would fix

If a scheduled job or an integration solves it, that is the answer, and we will tell you rather than sell around it.

Fully autonomous, out of the box

Nothing goes straight to full autonomy. It runs in parallel first and earns the lead.

03Engagement

Six weeks, one process

Week 1

Map the wiring

We shadow the process as it's actually run today, not the flowchart version, and find where it breaks.

YOU GIVE US

Two hours with the person who runs it. System access, read-only.

Week 2–3

Design the agent

We define the agent's goal, guardrails, and escalation points with the team who owns the process.

YOU GIVE US

One decision-maker who can set tolerances and sign off on the escalation rules.

Week 4–6

Deploy in parallel

The agent runs alongside the existing process, building trust before it takes the lead.

YOU GIVE US

Write access when you're ready, not before. A weekly review of what it got wrong.

Handover

You own it

Configuration, documentation, and a working session. From then on your team tunes it and extends it.

YOU KEEP

The agent, the guardrails, the trail, and the right to change any of it without calling us.
