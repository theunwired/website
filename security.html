Security & data

Your systems, your data, your model account.

The agent is built inside your infrastructure and runs there after handover. This page sets out what we touch, when we touch it, and what we sign before any of it starts.

What you get
01 Where it runs

It lives in your infrastructure.

01 Your environment

The agent is deployed in your cloud or on your servers, under your access rules. We don't host it, and we don't keep a copy of it running anywhere else.

02 Your model account

The agent calls a language model through your own account with the provider you choose. Your documents go to a provider you already hold terms with, under those terms.

03 Your infrastructure bill

Hosting and model usage are billed to you directly by your providers. You see every line of it.

04 Maintenance is optional

After handover your team runs it. If you want us to maintain it, that is a separate agreement with its own billing. Nothing stops working if you never sign one.

02 Access

Access comes in steps, and you can take it back at any point.

Week 1

Read-only

We watch the process as it runs. We can read what the systems hold. We can't change any of it.

WE HOLD

Read-only access for named people on our side, listed in writing.

Week 2–3

No new access

Design happens with your process owner. Goals, guardrails and escalation points are agreed before the agent is allowed to act on anything.

WE HOLD

The same read-only access. Nothing added.

Week 4–6

Write access, on your sign-off

The agent runs in parallel with your team. Every action it takes is logged in your environment.

WE HOLD

Write access scoped to the systems this one process touches, granted when you approve it.

Handover

Our access ends

Every credential we held is revoked on the handover date, and you confirm it. If you sign a maintenance agreement, the access it needs is written into that agreement.

WE KEEP

No credentials and no copies of your data.

03 Your data

What happens to your data.

01 Samples during discovery

We may ask for sample documents to test against before the build. They're covered by the NDA, used only for that assessment, and deleted when it ends. We confirm the deletion in writing. If you'd rather samples never leave your systems, we run discovery inside your environment from day one.

02 Nothing leaves your systems

The agent reads from your systems and writes back to them. We don't export your data to storage of our own, during the build or after it.

03 No training on your data

We don't use your data to train anything. Your model provider's terms govern what it does with what it receives. We'll go through those terms with your team before the build starts.

04 The log stays with you

Every input the agent read and every action it took is recorded in your environment. Your auditors read the same log your team does.

04 Paperwork

What we sign before we start.

01 NDA

Signed before you share a single sample. Your template or ours, whichever your legal team prefers.

02 Data processing agreement

Available on request for any engagement that touches personal data.

03 Your vendor assessment

We fill in your security questionnaire as written. Where a question doesn't apply to a studio our size, the answer says so.

04 Incident notification

You set the notification window. Tell us your requirement and it goes into the contract as written. If something breaks, you hear what happened, what we're doing about it, and when it will be fixed.

What we don't claim

We are not SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified. Because the agent runs inside your infrastructure, it sits under the controls you already audit. What we can show you is the access we hold, the dates we hold it, and a log of everything the agent did.
