02 The handover test

If your team can't change it, we built it wrong.

Before we call a build done, someone on the client's team does three things on their own. Here is the same test on a working rule set. Edit the config and the queue reruns.

  1. 1Change a tolerance. Edit price_tolerance_pct or auto_post_max_amount.TO DO
  2. 2Add an escalation rule. Put a vendor on always_escalate_vendor. Try Kestrel Freight.TO DO
  3. 3Explain a decision. Find out why the invoice went to a person. Search the log and open it.TO DO

Passed. That is the whole test. A client's team does exactly this on their own system before we sign off, and if they can't, the build isn't finished.
