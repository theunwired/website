SHEET 00 · THE LESSONThe line matters. The rule matters more.

Illustrative. 200 made-up invoices, the same for every visitor. Money paid wrong counts the whole amount of a duplicate and only the overbilled part of an overbill. Only the price tolerance and the duplicate rule are on here, so the two can be seen alone.
