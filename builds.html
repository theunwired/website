Builds

What we can show you working in discovery.

These are the builds we can run against your own documents and systems as a demo, before you pay anything. They are patterns we know how to deliver. They are not client case studies. Those go on the Work page once a client has measured the result.

By industry
01 The builds

Ten patterns cover most of what operations teams ask for.

Most requests turn out to be one of these, or two of them joined together. Each one is shaped to your process, your systems and your tolerances. None of them ships as a product.

01 · IDP

Document extraction

Any document in, structured data out. Invoices, forms, IDs, contracts, claims. Every field gets a confidence score, and the fields below your threshold go to a review screen beside the source.

  • In: Scans, photos, PDFs and email attachments, in any layout
  • Out: Validated fields written into your system of record
How it works
A PERSON

Checks only the fields the model is unsure about, shown next to the source.

02 · FINANCE

AP invoice exceptions

Invoices that fail the three-way match. The agent finds the reason, pulls the PO, receipt and contract, and proposes the fix or drafts the vendor query.

  • In: Invoices your matching rules declined
  • Out: A resolution proposal with sources, or a vendor query sent and chased
Finance in detail
A PERSON

Approves the posting. Nothing posts on the agent's authority.

03 · RAG

Knowledge assistant

Ask a question in plain language and get an answer from your own policies, contracts, SOPs and past tickets, with the passage it came from. When the answer isn't in your documents, it says so.

  • In: Your document library, under the access rules it already has
  • Out: Cited answers in chat, Slack, Teams or your intranet
A PERSON

Reads the cited source before acting on it. The assistant answers questions. It doesn't make decisions.

04 · ANY TEAM

Inbox triage

A shared mailbox read, sorted and routed. The agent classifies each message, pulls out the order number, invoice or case ID, files it, and drafts the reply.

  • In: Shared inboxes for AP, orders or support
  • Out: Messages routed with their key fields extracted, and replies drafted
A PERSON

Sends the reply, or approves the rules for what can go out on its own.

05 · SUPPLY CHAIN

Sales order entry

Purchase orders from email and PDF turned into sales orders, checked against price lists and stock before they are created.

  • In: Customer POs in any format
  • Out: Sales orders in your ERP, or held with the mismatch named
Supply chain in detail
A PERSON

Clears the orders where the price, part or quantity does not match.

06 · FINANCE

Cash application

Payments matched to open invoices using the bank file, the remittance email and the ledger. Short and split payments come with a stated reason.

  • In: Bank statements, remittance advice and open receivables
  • Out: Proposed matches with the reasoning for each
Finance in detail
A PERSON

Approves the matches the agent is not sure about.

07 · LEGAL & FINANCE

Contract tracker

Key terms, dates and obligations pulled out of your contracts into a tracker, with reminders before each renewal or notice date.

  • In: Signed contracts and their amendments
  • Out: A searchable register of terms, each one linked to its clause
A PERSON

Reviews the extracted terms before they become the record.

08 · HR

Employee onboarding

One approved hire becomes accounts, payroll setup, equipment requests and a day-one checklist, across every system that needs it.

  • In: The approved hire record
  • Out: Accounts, requests and a checklist, all tracked to done
HR in detail
A PERSON

HR approves the record that starts it. Nothing moves before that.

09 · CUSTOMER OPS

Support case preparation

Each new case read, classified and assembled with the order, account, history and policy before anyone on your team opens it.

  • In: Cases from email, forms or chat
  • Out: A case ready to act on, with the proposed next step
Customer ops in detail
A PERSON

Handles the customer and approves anything that moves money.

10 · COMPLIANCE

Audit evidence collection

Evidence for each control pulled from the source systems on schedule and filed with its source, so the audit pack exists before the auditor asks.

  • In: Your control list and the systems that hold the evidence
  • Out: An evidence pack per control, with gaps flagged early
Compliance in detail
A PERSON

Tests the control and signs. The agent never concludes a control passed.

What a demo is

A discovery demo runs on your samples, not on ours, and it shows you where the build struggles as well as where it works. It is not the finished system. Production means your access rules, your tolerances, a parallel run and a handover, which is what the build phase is for.
