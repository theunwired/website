Customer operations

The work behind the ticket.

A chatbot can answer the question. Somebody still has to find the order, check the policy, issue the credit and update three systems. That second part is where cases stall, and it is where we build. Your customers still talk to your team.

All industries
01

Case triage and context

Highest volume

Before anyone on your team opens the case, the agent has read it, classified it, and pulled the order, the account, the history and the relevant policy into one view.

  • Intent and urgency classified from the first message
  • Account, order and prior cases gathered from every system
  • Cases routed to the right queue with the reason shown
02

Refunds, credits and returns

Strong fit

The policy is written down. Applying it means checking eligibility, amounts and history across systems. The agent prepares the decision. A person approves anything that moves money.

  • Eligibility checked against the policy as written
  • Credit or refund prepared in the billing system and held for approval
  • Return authorisation raised and tracked to receipt
03

Order and account changes

Strong fit

Address changes, plan changes, cancellations. Small requests that touch billing, fulfilment and the CRM separately. The agent makes the change everywhere once it is confirmed.

  • Changes applied across every system together after confirmation
  • Mismatched records flagged rather than overwritten
04

Follow-ups

Strong fit

Cases die waiting on a warehouse, a supplier or another team. The agent chases until the case closes, and keeps the customer informed.

  • Internal follow-ups sent and escalated on a schedule you set
  • Customer updates drafted for a person to send, or sent within rules you approve
05

Reply drafting

Draft only

The agent drafts a reply from the case facts and your knowledge base, and shows what it used. Your team edits it and sends it.

  • Drafts grounded in the policy and the account, with sources
  • Nothing sent on a sensitive case without a person
01 How the agent works

The agent prepares the case. A person handles the customer.

1 The case arrives

By email, form, chat or phone transcript. The agent reads it as it actually came in.

2 It gathers context

Order, account, history and the policy that applies, pulled from every system before anyone looks.

3 It prepares the action

The refund, the change or the reply, staged and ready, with the reasoning attached.

4 A person approves

Your team checks it and confirms. Every system updates together once they do.

Where we say no

We don't put an agent in front of your customers pretending to be a person. We don't build anything that decides a complaint, a legal claim or a vulnerable customer's case. Those go to your team with the facts assembled, every time.
